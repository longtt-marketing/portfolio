Fix the signal, sell the right offer, then concentrate budget

1

Measure the paid booking

Trace a test enquiry and payment to CRM. Check consent, duplicate events and Pixel/CAPI deduplication before bidding to a verified paid booking; use qualified leads temporarily only if needed.

2

Sell each offer where it runs

At Novena and Katong, lead with the S$30 assessment ("Not sure where to start?"); at the other centres, test the S$30 trial class against the screen time angle, by age band.

Concentrate only when data supports it

Start a narrow Search pilot at centres with capacity. Expand only after reliable results; account for booking lag, group compatible offers and retain centre-level reporting. The PDF includes the proposed pilot.
