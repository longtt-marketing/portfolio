02 · Measurement & problem solving

Find the gap.
Then change the campaign.

A tracking gap in the TeeTee funnel

CRM recorded 12–15 registrations/day while Meta showed 0–2. I traced the measurement issue to restrictive consent gating, including the server-side Lead, and revised the implementation on 21 October.

36 valid CRM enquiries→25 consented→25 unique Leads

In my 22–24 October review, 25/25 eligible enquiries matched. The 11 refusals generated zero marketing events. Browser and server events shared IDs to avoid double counting.

25/36 overall coverage (69.4%); consent explains the denominator. Reported 24/24 QA checks are a separate test population. Direct tags + server-side Lead; no production GTM container claimed.
