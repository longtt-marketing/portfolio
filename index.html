Investigating a tracking discrepancy

When CRM registrations and Meta Lead events did not match, I reviewed the implementation and identified consent settings affecting both browser and server-side events.

After the revision, I reviewed consent-eligible records and duplicate handling. I keep enquiry, booking and payment definitions separate when reporting.
