How I work

From a weak signal
to a useful next step.

The part I enjoy is joining the dots: what the campaign says, what the customer does and what the team can improve next.

01

Read the situation

Start with the business goal, offer, audience and the question the campaign needs to answer.

02

Make the work

Set up or review the campaign, brief the creative, check the page and keep source information useful.

03

Close the loop

Compare platform activity with CRM and customer feedback, then explain the next test in plain language.

A real optimisation example

When a chat creative used the wrong objective, I rebuilt it as a Messages ad and moved existing budget to it.

It became July's strongest ad by conversation volume. I use this kind of review to decide what deserves the next test, while keeping conversations separate from customers and sales.
