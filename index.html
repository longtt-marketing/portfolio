Validate the handoff, then test a specific message

1

Measure the paid booking

24 planned tests across Meta/Search and desktop/mobile: consent granted/refused, invalid input, duplicates, unpaid booking and payment/refund. Require actual evidence, timestamp and reviewer. A WhatsApp opening remains diagnostic; paid booking requires a matched payment.

2

Test a clear parent question

Draft RDA hook: “Where should your child start with robotics?” Compare with “See how your child tackles a new challenge.” Keep the same offer, eligible locations and page; change the opening message only.

3

Concentrate only when data supports it

Start with one RDA intent group at centres with capacity. Keep brand, trial-class and holiday demand separate. The editable workbook includes 12 keyword drafts, 16 negative phrases and two ad concepts.

Match the offer to the parent question

Validate demand before allocating spend

08 · Proposed RDA pilot · SGD

A capped test with a commercial decision

One RDA offer at Novena/Katong, subject to capacity. Proposed targets and margins below need validation before launch.

S$6,000Total: S$3,000 media + S$2,000 management + S$300 follow-up + S$700 other costs
140 qualifiedBase target from 200 received enquiries
70 net paid74 paid bookings less four refunds; target 56 attendances
18 enrolledNet target; S$166.67 media-only CAC

Validate the economics first

Assume S$10 contribution per net RDA booking and S$450 incremental course contribution over 90 days. Base: 70 × 10 + 18 × 450 = S$8,800; less 20% reserve and S$6,000 costs leaves S$1,040, before fixed overhead.

Downside: −S$2,784; upside: +S$4,448. At S$300 course contribution the base is −S$1,120. These are conditional calculations, not profit forecasts. With 70 net bookings and S$450 contribution, 16 net enrolments break even; 17 provide a 10% cost buffer.

Release media against evidence

Prepare 2–6 Nov; live preflight 9 Nov. Acquire 9–29 Nov with weekly caps S$900 / S$1,000 / S$1,100. Allocate Meta S$1,800 including eligible remarketing; Search S$1,200.

Plan 96 gross-booking places over six service weeks. Centre/finance owners confirm capacity and margins. Pause affected spend for tracking, consent or payment failures; reconcile at 90% of the period cap. Close outcomes 29 Dec, then the latest 90-day contribution window on 29 Mar 2027.

Scale gate: mature cohort, at least 30 net paid bookings, validated margins, paid CPA ≤S$45, attendance ≥80%, enrolment/attendance ≥30%, at least 10% cost buffer and available capacity. This is an operating rule, not statistical proof or an automatic platform bid setting.
