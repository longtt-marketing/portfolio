06 · What I would change first, in order

Validate the handoff, then test a specific message

1

Measure the paid booking

Six proposed checks: consent allowed/refused, invalid input, duplicates, refresh and unpaid orders. A received enquiry maps to one CRM record. Marketing events require appropriate consent and deduplication; paid bookings need matched payments.

2

Test a clear parent question

Draft RDA hook: “Where should your child start with robotics?” Compare with “See how your child tackles a new challenge.” Keep the same offer, eligible locations and page; change the opening message only.

3

Concentrate only when data supports it

Start a narrow Search pilot at centres with capacity. Judge qualified enquiries and paid bookings after allowing for booking lag. Keep trial-class and assessment intent separate; the PDF includes draft Search copy.
