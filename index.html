03 / How I work

Stay curious.
Keep it practical.

I like to understand the problem before adding another campaign, tool or report.

01 — Understand

Get the brief right.

Agree on the goal, offer and audience. Read the existing campaign and listen to what sales and customer care are hearing.

02 — Make & check

Follow the whole journey.

Set up the campaign, brief the creative and check the page, form and tracking before drawing conclusions from the results.

03 — Learn & explain

Make the next step clear.

Use platform data and customer feedback to choose a useful test. Explain the decision in a short update the team can act on.
