What the landing page code does

  • Pixel on page view (with consent where needed)
  • Lead on form submit through Pixel and Conversions API, one event ID

  • WhatsApp tap secondary; Open House sign-up on your own site

07 · My first 30 days

Manage to one number: cost per paid discovery session, by centre and age

Week 1

Make the chain measurable

Audit GA4, Google Ads conversions, Pixel and CAPI; check how paid bookings are attributed and how many there are a month, then pick the most reliable booking or qualified-lead signal for each platform; one weekly sheet from lead to enrolment, by centre and campaign.

Week 2

Find the waste

Split spend and cost per booking by centre, programme, placement and search term; cut what brings chats but no bookings; add negatives.

Week 3

First tests, one variable each

Age-specific creatives, proof-led ads with your awards, and how the S$30 session is framed against free trials elsewhere.

Week 4

Decide, then plan the holidays

Keep, scale or stop each test on cost per paid booking; plan the next holiday-programme push; send a one-page report.

Every week from me

  • Spend, paid bookings, cost per booking, enrolments by centre
  • Three insights, each with the action taken
  • Tests running, decided and next
  • Tracking issues found and fixed

Working arrangement

  • Hanoi, GMT+7: one hour behind Singapore
  • Written updates in English, calls whenever needed
  • AI for research, copy variants and anomaly checks; every budget decision reviewed by me
