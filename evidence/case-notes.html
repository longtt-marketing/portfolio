Notes on the selected work

The portfolio focuses on responsibilities, decisions and evidence boundaries. These notes provide supporting scope for a recruiter who wants more detail.

ThreeTrees · Meta advertising

The selected Meta case covers a retained campaign record. Source: daily exports and monthly summaries. The figures are kept out of the main application because the decision and its limits matter more than a headline total.

The July optimisation example involved correcting a creative/objective mismatch and reallocating existing budget. Daily conversation volume improved at unchanged daily spend. This is an operational before/after comparison, not a controlled causal test. Conversations are not confirmed sales.

My role covered objectives, budgets, campaign and creative decisions, and reporting, with two media buyers operating the accounts alongside me.

TeeTee&Co. · registration and tracking

The registration record is kept separate from paid workshop bookings and customers.

I owned landing pages, forms, FluentCRM, source capture, email journeys and measurement. The October tracking review compared CRM registrations against platform events and investigated consent settings affecting browser and server-side Lead events.

The post-fix observations are candidate-confirmed; raw QA logs and platform exports are not attached. Review covered consent eligibility and duplicate handling. The implementation used direct tags and server-side events, not a claimed production GTM container.

ThreeTrees · Google test

The retained Search and Performance Max account record is a small test used to assess setup, search intent and conversion definitions.

Recorded conversion goals included contact and directions actions. They are not evidence of qualified enquiries or paid bookings. This was a small campaign test, alongside separate website and local-search implementation work.
