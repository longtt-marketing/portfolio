Education acquisition · July–August 2026

Source scope

Figures, dates, actions and reconciliation were confirmed by the candidate on 26–27 October 2026. This page is a summary of that account. Original Ads, CRM and payment exports are not attached, and the figures have not been independently verified.

Period and attribution

Enquiries acquired 1–31 July versus 1–31 August 2026, Vietnam time. Each enquiry receives the same 30-day outcome window. Data freeze: 31 August for the July cohort and 1 October for August. Later outcomes are kept separate.

Each unique person/offer enquiry is assigned to the first eligible paid touch. Assisted touches remain separate; unknown sources are not forced into Meta or Search. The channel rows below are components of one campaign.

Definitions

  • Qualified: correct offer/age, serviceable location, valid received contact and stated interest confirmed by sales; no duplicate or spam.
  • Net paid booking: a matched introductory-session payment, excluding refunded bookings.
  • Net enrolment: a paid programme enrolment, excluding cancellations/refunds.
  • Media CAC: media spend divided by net enrolments. It excludes management fees, overhead and delivery costs.

Role and limits

The candidate led budget, targeting, keyword exclusions, creative briefs and landing-page/CRM coordination. The centre team handled qualification, payments, refunds, attendance and enrolment. The comparison is observational; it does not establish the causal impact of individual changes or profit.
